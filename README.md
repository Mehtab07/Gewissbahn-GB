# Gewissbahn-GB

## The Problem

When planning a train journey with transfers, the fastest-looking route on paper isn't always the most reliable one in practice. A tight connection can look great on a timetable and still leave you stranded if the first train runs even a few minutes late. Standard journey planners rank routes by scheduled speed alone, without telling you how likely you are to actually make each transfer.

## The Solution

Gewissbahn-GB ranks train journeys across Germany by reliability, not just speed. For every route option, it looks at how that specific transfer has historically performed, checks current real-time delays, and gives a plain-language recommendation and a chatbot — so you can choose the connection you're actually likely to make, not just the one that looks fastest on the timetable.
